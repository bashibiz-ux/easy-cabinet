A plain panel. Vertical it stands upright (width x height); horizontal it lies flat (width x depth). Insert puts it after the last unit (or the one selected); move it where it goes. Select it to change its size.

Panels under the wall units that have a Carcass bottom raise upward, laid one after another along the whole run, like the toe kick. Each panel is as deep as the carcass less the front setback (20 mm to start); length 0 = one panel for the whole run.

Insert puts the panels under the wall units (the carcass rises by the panel thickness); Delete takes the selected ones away, or all of them when nothing is selected.

The pieces are laid end to end. Select a cabinet first and the run starts under its front; select a toe kick and the new one carries on from its end. Its height is the Toe-kick Height above, which also sets how far new cabinets stand off the floor.

Lays a worktop over the base units: one slab over each row of units standing together, and one in the shape of the corner on a corner unit. The front edge of a row's slab is rounded. Change a value and the worktop follows.

Select a cabinet in the model, enter the angle and press Rotate. It turns about the middle of its footprint; positive is clockwise seen from above.

Draws a floor, walls and a roof round the kitchen in the model, as groups of their own (the floor, walls, roof, doors and windows each on a tag of their own: Room Floor, Room Walls, Room Roof, Room Doors, Room Windows), with the door and windows you ask for cut through the walls, each in a white frame (a panel door with its handle, a casement window with its glass, handle and sills). Move a door or window with the Move tool: it goes to the nearest wall, turns parallel to it, and the opening in the wall moves with it. Copy and paste one (or move a copy with Ctrl) and the copy gets an opening of the same size where it lands. Delete one and the wall is closed there. Update Room does it if it did not happen by itself. A wall stands right behind the cabinets on every side their backs are against; on a side without backs (the open side of a straight kitchen) the wall is the open-side distance below away, one for the x (red) and one for the y (green) direction. A wall is measured along its inside from its start: the south and north walls from the west end, the west and east walls from the south end. Build Room draws it again; Remove Room takes it away.
