By unit type: a base unit's handle sits near the top of its door, a wall unit's near the bottom, a tall unit's at hand height. Offsets are the clear distance from the door edge to the handle.

The Base Unit door material also applies to the corner unit. Fillers follow the door material; end panels and toe kicks do too unless you pick their own.

A wall unit hangs at height 0, so the toe-kick height does not raise it.

Enter the size, select the cabinet, then Insert. The filler goes on the cabinet's wall side with its front flush with the door, at the same height off the floor as the cabinet. The height starts as the selected cabinet's, and the filler takes that cabinet's door material (see Material).

Select a cabinet or filler first: the end panel attaches to its exposed side. Depth starts flush with the door front and height at the top of what you selected; both can be changed.

The pieces are laid end to end. Select a cabinet first and the run starts under its front; select a toe kick and the new one carries on from its end. Its height is the Toe-kick Height above, which also sets how far new cabinets stand off the floor.

Lays a worktop over the base units: one slab over each row of units standing together, and one in the shape of the corner on a corner unit. The front edge of a row's slab is rounded. Change a value and the worktop follows.

Cutting: enter the longest piece (0 = no cutting) to cut every row into equal-length pieces, or list distances from the start of each row to cut exactly there. Back splash 0 = none.

Wall bottom panel - panels under the wall units that have a Carcass bottom raise upward, laid one after another along the whole run, like the toe kick. Depth 0 = as deep as the carcass; length 0 = one panel for the whole run.

Select a cabinet in the model, enter the angle and press Rotate. It turns about the middle of its footprint; positive is clockwise seen from above.
