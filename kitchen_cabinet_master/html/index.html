Enter the size, select the cabinet, then Insert. The filler goes on the cabinet's wall side with its front flush with the door, at the same height off the floor as the cabinet. The height starts as the selected cabinet's, and the filler takes that cabinet's door material (see Material).

Select a cabinet or filler first: the side panel attaches to its exposed side. Depth starts flush with the door front and height at the top of what you selected; both can be changed.

A plain panel. Vertical it stands upright (width x height); horizontal it lies flat (width x depth). Insert puts it after the last unit (or the one selected); move it where it goes. Select it to change its size.

Panels under the wall units that have a Carcass bottom raise upward, laid one after another along the whole run, like the toe kick. Each panel is as deep as the carcass less the front setback (20 mm to start); length 0 = one panel for the whole run.

Insert puts the panels under the wall units (the carcass rises by the panel thickness); Delete takes the selected ones away, or all of them when nothing is selected.

The pieces are laid end to end. Select a cabinet first and the run starts under its front; select a toe kick and the new one carries on from its end. Its height is the Toe-kick Height above, which also sets how far new cabinets stand off the floor.

Lays a worktop over the base units: one slab over each row of units standing together, and one in the shape of the corner on a corner unit. The front edge of a row's slab is rounded. Change a value and the worktop follows.
