Getting started

Install the .rbz file with Extensions > Extension Manager > Install Extension. The new version appears at once: you do not have to close SketchUp. Open the panel from Extensions > Easy Cabinet. You can dock it beside the model or leave it floating.

The panel is a stack of cards: Settings, Models, Auto Kitchen, Handle, My Appliances, Material, Cabinets and Extras, Worktop, Visibility, Rotate, Room and Cut List. Click a card title to open or close it. The buttons at the bottom (Insert, Update, At Point and the others) are always there.

All sizes are in the unit chosen in Settings (millimetres, centimetres, inches and so on). Change the unit and every field changes with it.

Placing cabinets

From the gallery

Open Cabinets and Extras. Choose a Category (Base, Wall or Tall), and the gallery below shows every cabinet of that category as a picture, in 3D or 2D, in three sizes. Pictures follow the model you chose: with handles, with a Gola profile, or without handles.

  1. Type in the search field above the gallery to find a cabinet by name.
  2. Click a cabinet. It follows your cursor in the model.
  3. Click where it goes. On an edge, its back stands against that edge and its front faces you.
  4. Use the ← and → keys to turn it a quarter, and Esc to drop it.

A cabinet placed this way stands on its toe kick. Drag inside the gallery, or use the mouse wheel, to scroll it.

With the Insert button

Choose a cabinet in the list, set its fields, and press Insert. It is placed next to the last cabinet or at the start of the row. At Point works like the gallery click for the cabinet shown in the panel. On Path uses the edges you selected as walls and places the cabinet again and again along them.

Changing a cabinet

Select a cabinet in the model: its settings open in the panel. Edit a field and the cabinet changes at once (the live tick in Settings). Press Update to apply all fields in one go.

  • Size: width, depth, height and the number of shelves. A resize can move the neighbours and side panels with it.
  • Doors and drawers: door gap, reveal and distance, front edge radius (0 gives square edges), opening angle.
  • Adjustable heights: some cabinets have their own fields, such as the lower door height, the top door of a fridge unit, the width of a blind panel or the heights of the drawers.
  • Duplicate copies the selected cabinet. Delete removes it. Undo goes back one step.
  • Toggle opens and closes the doors and drawers.

Models and handles

The Models card chooses the catalog:

Kitchen Cabinet - with handleDoors and drawers with a handle of your choice.
Kitchen Cabinet - Gola handleA Gola profile instead of a handle (types B, C, D and E).
Kitchen Cabinet - without handleA finger gap over the doors and drawers.
WardrobeHinged, sliding, open and corner wardrobes. See below.

The Handle card picks the handle, its length, finish, position and direction. The Material card sets the colours and boards of the carcass and the fronts; materials can also come from your own model. Front Design gives the fronts a flat, shaker, fluted or other look, and a glass door option is available.

Your own cabinets can be saved to a catalog of yours (My cabinets) and used again.

Inside the wardrobe

  • Dividers (0 to 5) split the wardrobe into sections of the same width. Sections narrower than 250 mm are refused.
  • Hanging rail: when ticked, the 1st, 3rd and so on sections hang clothes, with a round rail under a shelf. The other sections have shelves.
  • Shelves sets how many shelves each shelf section has.

Sliding doors open and close with Toggle: the doors in the front track run along the ones behind. The gallery shows the open wardrobes with garments and folded towels, for the picture only.

Fillers and panels

Besides cabinets, the Category list has these extras. Each one has its own picture in the gallery; click it to choose it, set its fields, and press Insert.

  • Filler: a board beside a cabinet that closes a gap.
  • Side Panel: covers the side of a cabinet. With the side panels switch on, panels are placed where a side is open, and they move with the cabinet when it is resized.
  • Toe Kick: the plinth under the base cabinets.
  • Wall Bottom Panel: a panel under the wall units.
  • Panel: a plain board of any size.
  • Ceiling Filler: closes the gap between the tall or wall units and the ceiling.

The Worktop card puts a worktop and a back splash over the base cabinets, with the front and end overhang and the edge you choose. Loft units sit over the wall and tall units.

Rooms and kitchens

Room

The Room card builds the walls of a room from their lengths, with a door and up to two windows, and can remove or update it later. Each wall, door and window has its own size and position.

Auto Kitchen

The Auto Kitchen card designs a kitchen for the room you built (or along the edges you selected): you answer a few questions, such as the layout, the appliances and the units you want, and the cabinets, appliances, worktop and wall units are placed for you. Change an answer and the kitchen is built again. You can mirror the kitchen and turn the units round.

Appliances and sinks

The Visibility card shows or hides the carcass, doors, toe kicks, worktop, appliances, handles, names and the door direction symbols. The My Appliances card adds your own appliance models.

Dimensions and LayOut

  • Dimensions: tick it in Settings and the plan (width and depth) and height dimensions are drawn for the cabinets and kept up to date.
  • LayOut scenes: the Room card can create scenes (plan, elevations and 3D views) ready to send to LayOut. In LayOut, insert the model and choose one scene for each viewport.
  • The Door direction symbols on the plan and on the elevations can be shown or hidden in Visibility.

Cut list

The Cut List card lists every part of the cabinets by material and thickness, with its length, width and number. You can group the parts, show names, and export the list to a file for your saw or for a cutting optimiser.

Help and feedback

Press More at the bottom of the panel:

  • Help shows a short guide and a button to open this page.
  • Feedback writes a comment, a suggestion or a problem report. It is sent from your own mail program (or Gmail, Outlook or Yahoo in your browser), and you can add the version of Easy Cabinet and SketchUp.
  • Donate opens the donation page. Easy Cabinet is made by one person, and every donation helps to keep it going.

If something does not work, send a problem report with what you did, the cabinet you used, and the version shown in Extensions > Extension Manager.
